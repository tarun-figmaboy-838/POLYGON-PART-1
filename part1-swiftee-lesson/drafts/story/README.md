# The Momo and Popo story — parked

The five-scene story that opened Part 1 (Momo and Popo, before Swiftee's first screen) is
**not in the game**. It was taken out on 2026-10-01 at the user's request ("remove the story
part and add on draft") and kept here, whole and unchanged, so it can come back.

Without it, Start goes straight to Swiftee's screen 1. Nothing here is deployed
(`.vercelignore` excludes `part1-swiftee-lesson/drafts`).

## What is here

Each file sits at the same path it had under `part1-swiftee-lesson/`, moved with `git mv`:

| here | was |
|---|---|
| `src/story/story.js` | the player: scenes, bubbles, word-by-word reveal, motion cues |
| `src/story/story-data.js` | the script and its staging (lines, speakers, voice ids, cues) |
| `src/story/story-art.js` | the art list (generated by `tools/build-story.js`) |
| `src/audio/story-music.js` | the story's music bed |
| `tools/build-story.js` | writes `src/story/story-art.js` |
| `assets/story/*` | the five paintings and their surrounds, and the music |
| `assets/vo/st*-*.mp3, .ogg` | the eight story voice clips (narrator, Momo, Popo) |
| `story.css` | its styles, lifted verbatim out of `index.html` |

Still in the game folder, because they are sources rather than the story itself:
the recordings `assets/source/vo-masters/narrator.mp3` and `popo.mp3`, their cue maps
`docs/vo-masters/narrator.json` and `popo.json`, and the story's word timings in
`assets/vo/word-timings.json`.

## To bring it back

1. Move every file here back to the same path under `part1-swiftee-lesson/` (`git mv`).
2. In `index.html`:
   - paste `story.css` (without its first comment) back into `<style>`, just before `</style>`;
   - put back `<div id="story" hidden></div>` just before the Part 2 button's comment;
   - put back `<script src="src/audio/story-music.js"></script>` after `src/audio/music.js`;
   - put back, just before `src/game/game.js`:
     `src/story/story-art.js`, `src/story/story-data.js`, `src/story/story.js`.
3. Rebuild: `node tools/build-vo-index.js`, `npm run vo:script`, `node tools/build-preload.js`.
4. Bring back the story checks in `tests/playthrough.jsdom.js` (its five scenes, its script word
   for word, no Next button). They are in commit `5c75347`, the last one with the story in.

`src/game/game.js` was not changed. Every place it uses the story is already guarded
(`global.Story && Story.enabled()`), so the game runs with or without it.
