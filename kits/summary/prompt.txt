THE METHOD TO KEEP (this is what makes it work; don't simplify it away)
- One card per idea the lesson TAUGHT, in the order it was taught, as data: id, label, one short
  line, its recording, and what the card shows. One card component draws every idea.
- A strict state sequence per card: CARD_ENTER → CONCEPT_REVEAL → PRESENTER_ENTER → EXPLANATION
  → READING_PAUSE → PRESENTER_EXIT → CARD_COLLECT → NEXT_CONCEPT … → FINAL_SUMMARY → READY.
  Each step waits for the one before it: no two states overlap, no voice plays over another,
  nothing can be tapped while a line is being said.
- The words appear on the recording's own word timings, and the key word lights the part of the
  card it names. With no recording, pace the words (~330 ms each).
- Cards collect into albums at the sides; the finale gathers them; READY shows Next, and a tap
  on a collected card replays its idea and line while the others rest dimmed.
- prefers-reduced-motion: the same states, without the motion.
