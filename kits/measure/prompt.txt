THE METHOD TO KEEP (this is what makes it work; don't simplify it away)
- SIDES: the child taps a side; Swiftee walks along that side with the tape; its length appears
  only when he reaches the end, and a sparkle pops it in. Taps made while he walks are queued and
  measured in turn, each side once (a double tap counts once). When the last side is in, the
  equal-side ticks are dealt round the shape one after another.
- ANGLES: Swiftee walks round the corners in order carrying ONE protractor, the same one from
  corner to corner, never re-created. At each corner its centre sits exactly on the vertex and
  its baseline exactly along a side; he holds it there while the degrees appear. His whole body is
  always shown (never clipped or masked), kept near upright, never squashed. Every reading stays
  bright once shown.
- While he walks, his usual figure is hidden; it comes back when the walk ends.
- Leaving the scene mid-walk cancels it cleanly: nothing is left on screen and no reading
  appears later on the next scene.
- prefers-reduced-motion: the same readings, shown at once, without the walk.
- It works at any screen size: the walk is placed from the shape's real corners, not fixed pixels.
